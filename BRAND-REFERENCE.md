# Emmanuel Baptist Church brand reference

Source: https://live.standards.site/emmanuel

## Brand colors
- Dark Teal: `#044F56` (dominant)
- Gold: `#FFAE05` (accent only)
- Near Black: `#232323`
- Light Teal: `#7BC2C8`
- Cream: `#EDE0CA`
- White: `#FFFFFF`

## Typography
- Meno Banner Bold: large display titles; **not** body text. Must verify rights before hosting font files publicly.
- Montserrat Bold: H1; Semibold: H2, navigation and labels; Regular: body at minimum 12px digitally and 1.5 line height.

## Official marks
Use official supplied variations; clear space ≥¼ inch when practical. Do not stretch, rotate, outline, shadow, or recolor in unapproved colors. Digital minimum width for logo lockups: 120px where legibility permits.

## Voice and imagery
Rooted, Warm, Alive, Honest. Plain, specific, personal language. For church life photos, use genuine Emmanuel moments only. Factual, properly licensed location photos are suitable for this DC travel guide.
